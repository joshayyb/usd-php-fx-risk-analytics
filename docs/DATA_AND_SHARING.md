# Data, privacy and sharing review — checked11 November2026

## Source-based decisions

BSP's [Terms of Use](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Pages/AboutTheBank/Terms-of-Use.aspx) permit reproduction with credit and describe conditions for commercial redistribution/derivatives, including source attribution, identifying modifications, due diligence and explaining that data are freely retrievable. They reserve separate treatment for BSP logos/currency facsimiles and third-party-licensed content. This is a review of the published terms, not an independent legal clearance or a blanket license for every component.

The package credits BSP, identifies the processed extract and its modifications, retains original row/hash lineage, and states that the source is freely available. No BSP logo or currency image is used. The exact historical field is the USD row's PHIL. PESO EQUIVALENT; no claim is made that every historical rate is an executable close or that general reference-rate metadata fully explains the series. Check any upstream third-party rights before final publication.

[BPI's official integrated-report listing](https://www.bpi.com.ph/about-bpi/investor-relations/integrated-reports) establishes the disclosure source. The source report's SHA-256, Group scope, date, units and positive net were verified in the original workspace. It does not establish a blanket redistribution license or publication date. This package retains only factual disclosure metadata, citations and the project's static derived figures. The complete BPI PDF and logos are excluded; no endorsement, actual BPI VaR, total bank loss or risk appetite is claimed.

## Included

- Own implementation and curated narrative; cleaned notebook reading copies.
- Attributed BSP-derived interval extract and source audit metadata/links.
- Exact numerical result CSVs and chart previews; working dashboard with the same derived arrays.
- Source links and hashes rather than raw third-party PDFs.

## Excluded

- Original BSP workbook and source-bulletin PDFs: unnecessary raw distribution for this portable result replay; acquire separately if repeating the full source audit.
- Complete BPI integrated report PDF; unrelated loans workbook.
- DuckDB database, environments, caches, logs, personal browser exports, attachments, chat transcripts and screenshots exposing browser tabs or account information.
- Local absolute paths and saved notebook outputs; cleaned copies are labelled and do not replace the original evidence.
- Original full frozen manifests pointing to omitted raw-source files; this candidate uses its own PACKAGE_MANIFEST.json without pretending to be byte-identical to the complete research archive.
