## 5. Findings and permitted conclusions

- The USD1 million baseline starts at **PHP62.485 million**. Every 1% FX move changes its peso value by **PHP624,850**. A -3% shock produces a PHP1,874,550 loss; a -5% shock produces a PHP3,124,250 loss. Positive shocks produce equal gains under these fixed linear assumptions.
- The selected historical decline is **58.392 to 57.514 PHP/USD, 4-5 August 2025**, approximately **-1.5036%**, with a one-calendar-day source gap. Its percentage move is replayed on the September 2026 baseline. The original historical USD1m loss was PHP878,000; the replay differs because its starting valuation differs.
- The BPI static illustration changes by **PHP8.14 million per 1%**. A -3% shock implies approximately PHP24.42 million loss and -5% approximately PHP40.70 million loss. These are simplified changes to a disclosed net on-balance-sheet exposure, not actual bank earnings or total risk.
- Stress results answer what-if questions beyond a single quantile threshold. They do not fix VaR coverage weaknesses, establish likelihood, demonstrate avoided losses or imply that +/-5% bounds all possible outcomes.
- The two cases use different dates and exposure definitions. Do not combine them into a portfolio, compare bank risk rankings, or feed the BPI figures into the frozen VaR backtest.
